# Closed Tabs

Closed Tabs is a YouTube channel about real internet mysteries: puzzle hunts that
recruited strangers, broadcasts nobody has explained, channels that posted thousands
of videos and vanished, lost media, and the folklore the internet made up about
itself.

Every episode is written from at least two open sources - the Wikipedia article on
the subject and at least one independent page - and those sources are listed in
the description of the video. Where the sources disagree or do not know, the
episode says so.

## What the channel never covers

Real deaths or suicides, victims of crimes, private individuals, minors, active
criminal cases, or graphic detail of any kind.

## How the videos are made

- Narration is a synthetic voice (Kokoro, Apache-2.0). No real person's voice is
  cloned or imitated.
- Imagery is generated (FLUX.1-schnell, Apache-2.0) or drawn in code, and is
  illustrative, not archival. No real people are depicted.
- Music is by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com) under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0,
  credited in every description.
- Every video declares itself as containing synthetic media.

The production code is public: <https://github.com/edbackulin-eng/closed-tabs>
